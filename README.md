## 👋 About Me

I'm **Niranjan Epili** — Founder & CEO of **NexScope Studio**, and a **BTech CSE (AI/ML)** student at Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj University, Panvel, Mumbai.

NexScope is a service-based agency under **NCompany** (my parent company). We build **websites, Shopify stores, AI agents & automation**, and run **performance marketing** — everything under one roof, so clients don't have to hire ten different people.

- 👥 **10+ member team** across India
- 🤝 **5+ growth partnerships** with startups, influencers, personal brands, LLPs & enterprises
- 🎯 One goal in every project: **save time, cut cost, drive growth**
